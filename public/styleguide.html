00 / Direction

A gentle
Caregiver.

The primary visitor's class-day need leads this first draft.

Audience hypotheses, not research

Audience

A person with a class-only day, looking for simple choices. Professional-event wear is a second, unvalidated hypothesis.

Archetype and voice

Caregiver / gentle, comforting, and casual.

Visual direction

Modernist, minimalist and simple. The lighter feel describes the page, not the shirt's physical weight or comfort.

07 / Persuasion

Helpful, not
pushy.

Only honest, optional ideas belong on this page.

Reciprocity / free outfit idea

“For a simple class day, pair the simple shirt with something you already like wearing.”

The tip is visible without choosing a size or adding to the demo bag. It offers a simple styling prompt with no product claim and no return action required.

Liking / warm voice

“When in simple day, ComfyCloth is here for you”

Gentle wording and the shared simple-day situation make the page feel welcoming. It is an invitation, not a claim about the shirt's physical comfort.
